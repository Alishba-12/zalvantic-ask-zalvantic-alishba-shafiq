# Privacy Policy | Zalvantic

Source: https://zalvantic.com/privacy

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, company name, and project details when you fill out our contact form. We may also collect usage data through analytics tools to improve our website experience.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, provide our services, and communicate relevant updates. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS. We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy.

## Cookies

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies (such as _ga) to measure how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are viewed and how long visits last. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, or opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

## Third-Party Services

We use Resend to deliver contact form emails, Google Analytics to understand website usage, and Cloudflare to host the website, protect it from abuse, and verify that form submissions come from real people (Cloudflare Turnstile). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@zalvantic.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@zalvantic.com or through our contact form.
