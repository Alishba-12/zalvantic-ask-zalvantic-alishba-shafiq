# My notes on Zalvantic services

Source: my own notes from public sources (zalvantic.com pages: home, about, services, work, contact, terms, privacy)

## Who Zalvantic is

Zalvantic is a software house that helps businesses around the world build custom software, AI integration and UI/UX design. The founder and CEO is Zaid Ullah, and the team is small, remote-first and made of engineers, designers and AI specialists. The website says they have delivered 20+ projects, have 4+ years of experience and answer every enquiry within 48 hours.

## AI & Automation

Under AI & Automation, Zalvantic puts AI and automation inside a client product or daily operations. The AI Integration service covers chatbots with large language models (OpenAI and Anthropic), RAG systems, computer vision, predictive analytics, AI workflow automation and asking questions to data in plain language.

The Business Automation service is for repetitive work, like workflow automation, data entry, CRM and ERP integration, custom scripts and bots, email notifications and automatic reports.

Examples of this work on the website are an AI Tourism Assistant built with OpenAI and LangChain, and an Email Automation Agent that drafts, schedules and sends personal emails.

## Custom Web & Backend

For custom web and backend work, Zalvantic offers Custom Software Development, Web Development, SaaS Development, API Development and Software Development, which also includes mobile apps for iOS and Android. On the backend side they build REST and GraphQL APIs, database architecture, microservices and login security with OAuth and JWT. They also have separate service pages for Django, FastAPI and Flask development in Python.

Web work includes responsive and SEO friendly websites, CMS integration with WordPress and Strapi, e-commerce with Shopify and WooCommerce, and progressive web apps.

Projects I found on the website: the Big Bite Fast Food website (Next.js and React), StudyBud (Node.js and Socket.IO), the MIMS medicine inventory system (Django and MySQL), KBook (Python and PostgreSQL), and cloud infrastructure work with Linux, Nginx and Docker.

Besides these, the website also lists UI/UX design, branding, digital solutions and technical consulting.

## How a client can start

A client can email hello@zalvantic.com, send a WhatsApp message to +92 336 5856052, or fill in the contact form on the website. The website promises a reply within 48 hours, and a client does not need a full brief, because just an idea is enough to begin.

## Terms I noticed

Prices are not published on the website. Payment terms are written in each project agreement, and a deposit is usually needed before work starts. After full payment the client owns the custom work, and Zalvantic keeps all client information confidential. Zalvantic may show finished work in its portfolio unless the client says no in writing. The website also says security is built in from day one, with HTTPS, validated inputs and least privilege access.
